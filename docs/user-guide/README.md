## Getting Started

### What is Farm Commons?

Farm Commons is community-owned, open-source farm management software designed for small-scale farmers and farmworkers. It helps you manage:

- **Workers** - Staff directory with skills, certifications, and contact information
- **Schedules** - Daily work assignments and field-based task scheduling
- **Time Tracking** - Clock in/out functionality with compliance tracking
- **Dashboard** - Overview of farm operations and key metrics

### Who is Farm Commons for?

Farm Commons is designed for:

- **Small to medium-sized farms** (5-500 acres)
- **Organic and regenerative agriculture operations**
- **Farm cooperatives** and CSA programs
- **Farmworkers** who need transparent scheduling and wage tracking
- **Farm managers** who need to coordinate labor and compliance

## Accessing Farm Commons

### Web Application

Farm Commons is accessible through your web browser:

1. **Navigate to your Farm Commons URL** (e.g., https://farm-commons.example.com or http://localhost:5173 for local development)
2. **Log in** with your credentials
3. **Start managing** your farm operations

### System Requirements

- **Modern web browser** (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge)
- **Internet connection** (offline support coming in future phases)
- **Mobile-friendly** - Works on phones and tablets

---

## First Time Login

### Logging In

1. Open Farm Commons in your web browser
2. Enter your **email address** and **password**
3. Click **"Sign In"**

If you don't have an account yet, contact your farm administrator to create one for you.

### Understanding Your Dashboard

After logging in, you'll see the **Dashboard** - your home base for farm operations.

The Dashboard shows:

- **Active workers** currently clocked in
- **Today's schedules** and assignments
- **Pending time entries** that need verification
- **Quick actions** to create schedules or manage workers

## User Roles

Farm Commons has different user roles with varying permissions:

### Worker
- View own schedules
- Clock in and out
- View own time entries
- Update personal contact information

### Manager
- All Worker permissions
- Create and edit schedules
- Manage workers
- Verify time entries
- View farm-wide reports

### Admin
- All Manager permissions
- Manage user accounts
- Configure farm settings
- Access all data and reports

## Quick Start Workflows

### For Farm Managers

**Your typical workflow:**

1. **Morning:** Check Dashboard for today's schedules and active workers
2. **Plan work:** Create or update schedules for the day/week
3. **Assign workers:** Match workers to fields and tasks
4. **End of day:** Verify time entries and approve hours
5. **Weekly:** Review labor hours and generate reports

### For Farmworkers

**Your typical workflow:**

1. **Start of shift:** Clock in when you arrive
2. **Check schedule:** View your assigned tasks and fields
3. **During work:** Complete assigned tasks
4. **Breaks:** Clock out for breaks (if required)
5. **End of shift:** Clock out when leaving

## Data Privacy & Security

### Your Data is Private

Farm Commons is built with privacy first:

- **Your data stays with you** - Never sold to corporations
- **Worker data is protected** - Only authorized users can access personal information
- **Secure authentication** - Industry-standard password encryption
- **Audit logging** - Track who accessed what data

### Best Practices

- **Use strong passwords** - At least 8 characters with mix of letters, numbers, and symbols
- **Don't share accounts** - Each person should have their own login
- **Log out on shared devices** - Always log out when using shared computers
- **Report suspicious activity** - Contact your farm administrator if something seems wrong
